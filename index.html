1.Overview

Number Path: Calculate Your Escape (the “App”) is an offline math puzzle game made by AARCHEE SHARMA (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not sell personal information.

2.Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your own device (in the app’s local storage):

  • game progress, stars, unlocked skins, statistics and achievements;
  • your settings (sound, vibration, theme and similar choices);
  • your hint count, daily-challenge streak and the number of rewarded ads watched today;
  • the date and version of your acceptance of this Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions.

This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you use “Reset progress” in Settings, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the App.

3.Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and secure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, app interaction and diagnostic data, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and we do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Ads never appear while you are solving a puzzle. Interstitial ads appear only between levels.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. A reward (for example extra hints or moves) is given only after you finish watching the ad.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) so you can choose how your data is used for ads.

You can control ad personalisation and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Learn more: How Google uses data for ads and Google Privacy Policy.

4.Other third-party services

  • Google Play services are used on Android to run ads and consent.
  • Google Fonts: when you are online, the App may load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device contacts Google servers (your IP address is visible to them). Offline, the App uses a built-in font instead.

These services have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

5.In-app purchases

The App may offer optional purchases (such as removing ads) through Google Play Billing in the future. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment card details.

6.Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age set by the law in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7.Data retention and deletion

We do not keep your game data on any server. Data stored on your device stays there until you reset progress, clear app data or uninstall the App. Data held by Google for advertising is governed by Google’s policies and your Google account and device ad settings.

8.Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we do not transmit your game data to our own servers.

9.Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most choices are made on your device (reset progress, uninstall, ad settings, consent form). You may also write to us at the address below with any request or question.

10.International data transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own. Where required, Google applies safeguards for such transfers as described in its own policies.

11.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, the App may ask you to review and accept it again.
